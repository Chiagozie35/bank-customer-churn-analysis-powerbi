# Bank Customer Churn Analysis Using Power BI

## Project Overview

Customer churn is a major challenge for banks because losing customers directly impacts revenue and long-term profitability.

This Power BI project analyzes customer churn patterns across demographic, geographic, and behavioral factors to identify key drivers of customer attrition and support data-driven retention strategies.

## Business Problem

The bank wants to understand:

* Which customer segments are most likely to churn?
* What behaviors are associated with churn?
* Which customers should be targeted for retention efforts?

## Tools Used

* Power BI
* DAX
* Data Modeling
* Data Visualization
* Business Analytics

## Dataset Overview
The Dataset used is from https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/gauravtopre/bank-customer-churn-dataset?resource=download

## Key Metrics

* Total Customers
* Churned Customers
* Churn Rate
* Retention Rate
* Average Balance
* Average Salary

## Key Findings

* Overall churn rate is 20.4%.
* Inactive customers account for the majority of churn.
* Customers with only one product show the highest churn rate (69.2%).
* Germany records the highest churn rate (32.4%).
* Customers aged 40–59 exhibit the highest churn levels.
* Churned customers maintain higher average balances than retained customers.

## Business Recommendations

* Improve engagement strategies for inactive customers.
* Promote product bundling and cross-selling initiatives.
* Develop targeted retention campaigns for high-risk segments.
* Investigate factors driving churn in Germany.
* Strengthen loyalty programs for middle-aged customers.
